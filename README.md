# Credit Card Analytics & Reporting Dashboard — Power BI

##  Project Overview

Developed a **two-page interactive Credit Card Analytics & Reporting Dashboard** in Power BI to analyze credit card customer behavior, transaction performance, revenue generation, and demographic segments.

The project consists of two interactive report pages — **Customer Report** and **Transaction Report** — supported by DAX measures, KPI tracking, dynamic slicers, demographic analysis, card-category analysis, transaction-channel analysis, and customer/job-level insights.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of both **customer-level characteristics** and **transaction-level performance**, enabling users to explore business trends through interactive filtering and visual analysis.

---

##  Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

- How much revenue and transaction volume are being generated?
- How does revenue change across quarters and time periods?
- Which card categories generate the most revenue?
- Which transaction channels contribute the most revenue?
- How does customer revenue vary across job categories?
- Which customer demographics contribute most to revenue?
- How do income groups and education levels relate to customer spending?
- How does revenue vary across different states?
- How do customer segments differ by age, gender, marital status, and dependent count?
- What are the major revenue and transaction trends over time?

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Customer Report

The **Customer Report** focuses on customer demographics, income, spending behavior, and customer segmentation.

#### Key KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Interest
- Total Income
- Average Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS)

#### Visualizations & Analysis

- Revenue by Age Group and Gender
- Revenue trend by Year, Quarter, Month and Gender
- Customer Job-wise Revenue
- Transaction Amount by Customer Job
- Income by Customer Job
- Revenue by Top 5 States
- Revenue by Income Group
- Revenue by Dependent Count
- Revenue by Marital Status
- Revenue by Education Level

#### Customer Segmentation

Customers can be analyzed using dimensions such as:

- Age Group
- Gender
- Income Group
- Education Level
- Marital Status
- Dependent Count
- Customer Job
- State

This page helps identify differences in revenue contribution and transaction behavior across customer segments.

---

### 2. Transaction Report

The **Transaction Report** focuses on transaction volume, revenue, card categories, transaction channels, and expenditure patterns.

#### Key KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Transaction Amount
- Total Interest
- Total Transaction Count

#### Visualizations & Analysis

- Revenue & Transaction Volume by Quarter
- Revenue by Transaction Usage Type
- Revenue by Expenditure Type
- Revenue by Education Level
- Revenue by Customer Job
- Revenue by Card Category
- Card Category-wise Revenue, Interest Earned and Annual Fees

#### Transaction Usage Analysis

Transactions are analyzed across three major usage types:

- **Swipe**
- **Chip**
- **Online**

The dashboard allows comparison of revenue generated through different transaction channels.

##  Key Dashboard Features

### Interactive Slicers

The report includes interactive filters for dynamic analysis, including:

- Quarter
- Week Start Date
- Gender
- Income Category
- Customer Segments

Users can combine multiple filters to drill into specific customer or transaction segments.

### KPI Tracking

DAX-based KPI measures are used to monitor important business metrics including:

- Revenue
- Transaction Amount
- Transaction Count
- Interest Earned
- Income
- Average CSS
- Annual Fees

### Time-Based Analysis

The dashboard supports analysis across different time levels:

**Year → Quarter → Month → Day**

This enables users to analyze revenue and transaction trends over time.

### Demographic Analysis

Customer behavior is segmented using multiple demographic attributes:

- Age
- Gender
- Education
- Marital Status
- Income
- Dependents
- State
- Customer Job

### Card Performance Analysis

Card categories are compared based on:

- Revenue
- Interest Earned
- Annual Fees
- Transaction performance

The dashboard includes analysis across:

- Blue
- Silver
- Gold
- Platinum
